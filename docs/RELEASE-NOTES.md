# Release notes

## A title for every video

The first video in a session can now have its own title, just like the extra videos. Sessions without one look exactly as before.

## More than one video per session

Sessions can now hold more than one video. When a lesson has a second part, you'll see each video with its own title, one after another, and the reflection questions right below the last one. Sessions with a single video look and work exactly as before.
